How the numbers work

Load = outboard ÷ spread for sweep, outboard ÷ (spread ÷ 2) for sculling. Gearing = outboard ÷ inboard. Outboard = oar length − inboard. (Manual, Module 5, Ch. 2.)
Baseline uses the midpoint of each range in the manual's Figure 7 rigging table for the matched squad and boat. The manual's guidance is to start from the table, row, race and watch the boat, then adjust.
Crew weight picks the table. Men averaging 70 kg (154.3 lb) or less use the lightweight men's row; women averaging 57 kg (125.7 lb) or less use the lightweight women's row (World Rowing crew-average limits). Juniors use the junior tables. The manual does not scale load by body weight within a table, so this tool doesn't either: use the ladder to fit your crew's size and strength.
Inboard rule of thumb: sweep inboard = spread + 30 cm (±1); sculling inboard = (spread + 18 cm overlap) ÷ 2. Clams are about 1 cm each. Use the blade maker's recommendations for new blade shapes such as fat blades.
